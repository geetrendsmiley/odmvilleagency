ODM Ville website - odm-ville - HTML, CSS, JavaScript
1. Put your photo at images/odm-ville.jpg and a share image at images/og-share.jpg.
   The homepage slideshow can use four photos: odm-ville.jpg, odm-ville-2.jpg,
   odm-ville-3.jpg and odm-ville-4.jpg. It changes photos every five seconds;
   visitors can also choose a photo using the dots.
2. Edit js/main.js: set your WhatsApp number and email.
   The contact form opens a prefilled WhatsApp message; the visitor must tap Send.
   The email link opens the visitor's email app. Direct website delivery needs a form backend.
3. Replace content in the dashed placeholder boxes with real content.
4. Add verified social links in each page footer.
5. Replace yourdomain.com in sitemap.xml and robots.txt.
6. Open index.html, or upload the folder to any host.
